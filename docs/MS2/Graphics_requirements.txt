Requirements:

Default world size is 1280 x 720. Resizing should Keep the scale factor the same.


The map will consist of easy polygon shapes which will be drawn using JavaFX.
Golf Hole is an ellipse of size 40x20

Pool is a rect of size 230x250 
Bridge (weak green) is a rect of 230x20
Switch (gray-red) is an ellipse of size 50x30, opens/closes bridge
UI (dark blue) on right side is 200px wide
Ball is 30px Diameter
Breakable wall (red) is 30x70
Trees can be considered as 40x55 rects 
Bouncy balls (pink) have 50px Diameter (should increase ball Velocity)

Terrain
water (blue) ball should respawn at last position
ice (light blue) should have less friction
sand (yellow) should have more friction
Red walls are broken after two collisions
Brown walls are Unbreakable
The cats and trees break after one collision
